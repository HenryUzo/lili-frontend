# Pet Care Article Distribution Checklist

Use this checklist for every published Pet Care article.

- Search Console inspection completed.
- Google Business Profile post drafted.
- Brevo newsletter inclusion planned.
- Facebook post drafted.
- Instagram carousel drafted.
- Instagram Story drafted.
- Service-page internal link reviewed.
- Related-article links reviewed.
- UTM parameters added to distributed links.
- GA4 event verification completed.
- 30-day performance review scheduled.
- 90-day content update review scheduled.

Do not automate social posting in Phase 3.
